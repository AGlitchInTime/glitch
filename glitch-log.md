### 💥 March 2025  
**The case-sensitive disaster**  
Wrote `Gene` instead of `gene` in a column filter.  
Spent 45 minutes thinking Biopython hated me.  
🔁 Fix: Python doesn’t care about your pride — it cares about your cases.

---

### 🔬 February 2025  
**The incubator incident**  
Forgot to label two plates. Guessed which was which. Was wrong.  
🔁 Fix: Labels exist for a reason. So do regrets.

---

### 🧫 January 2025  
**The great contamination of ‘25**  
Accidentally touched the pipette tip. Tried to pretend it didn’t happen.  
Contaminated the whole batch.  
🔁 Fix: Don’t lie to your plates. They know.

---

### 🧠 December 2024  
**The “I'll remember this later” lie**  
Didn’t write down how I calculated the final concentration.  
Two weeks later, future-me suffered.  
🔁 Fix: Document everything. Even the dumb stuff. Especially the dumb stuff.
